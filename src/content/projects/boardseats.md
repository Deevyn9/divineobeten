## Overview

Boardseats came in needing a better website — one that matched the quality of their service. The existing site was outdated in both design and experience. The goal was a full redesign that felt modern, credible, and easy to navigate.

## What I Built

### Full Website Redesign

Redesigned and rebuilt 15+ screens across the entire site — overhauling the visual language, layout structure, and user flows from the ground up. Every page was touched with a focus on clarity, hierarchy, and a more polished aesthetic.

### Contact Form Integration

Connected and implemented contact forms across relevant pages — ensuring inquiries actually reached the right people without friction.

### UI & UX Improvements

Improved navigation structure, spacing, and component consistency throughout. The result was a site that felt significantly more professional and easier to use than what they started with.

## Outcome

Delivered a fully redesigned website with 15+ improved screens, connected contact forms, and a visual upgrade that gave Boardseats a stronger, more credible online presence.
