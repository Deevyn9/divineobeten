## Overview

Stunhaul is an ecommerce company specialising in gift items. As a full-time frontend role, I was responsible for building and maintaining the core shopping experience — from the landing page through to checkout.

## What I Built

### Landing Page & Marketing

Built the main landing page including hero sections, product showcases, and promotional carousels — designed to drive engagement and push users into the shopping flow.

### Product & Cart Experience

Built the full shopping list and cart experience — product listing views, item breakdowns, quantity management, and cart state management throughout the session.

### Checkout Flow

Built the checkout interface — form handling, order summary, and payment flow integration. Ensured the process was smooth, clear, and low-friction for customers completing purchases.

### Admin Interfaces

Built admin-facing interfaces for managing products, orders, and store operations — giving the internal team visibility and control over the platform.

### Forms & Interactions

Implemented forms and interactive elements across the site — ensuring data collection was smooth and the overall experience felt polished.

## Outcome

Delivered a complete ecommerce frontend covering the full customer journey — from landing page to checkout — plus the admin tooling needed to run the store day to day.
