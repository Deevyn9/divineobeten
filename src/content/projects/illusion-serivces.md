## Overview

Illusion Services is a tech consulting company that renders design, engineering, and various technology services to clients. They needed a website rebuild that properly communicated what they do, showcased their work, and told the story of the company and team behind it.

## What I Built

### Full Website Rebuild

Rebuilt the entire website from the ground up — replacing the existing site with a cleaner, more modern experience that better represented the quality of their services.

### Services & Work Showcase

Built out the services section communicating their full offering across design, engineering, and tech consulting — alongside a work showcase displaying past projects and capabilities.

### Company & Team Details

Built pages covering company background, mission, and team details — giving visitors the context and confidence to reach out and engage.

## Outcome

Delivered a fully rebuilt website that gave Illusion Services a stronger, more credible web presence — clearly communicating their services, work, and team in one cohesive experience.
