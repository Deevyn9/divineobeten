## Overview

SpaceHub is a coworking space company — providing professionals, remote workers, and teams with a physical space to work productively. They needed a landing page that communicated their offering clearly and made signing up feel effortless.

## What I Built

### Landing Page

Built the full landing page from design to deployment — communicating SpaceHub's value proposition, space offerings, and key details in a clean, conversion-focused layout.

### UI Implementation

Translated the design into a pixel-accurate, responsive implementation — ensuring the page looked and felt right across all screen sizes.

## Outcome

Delivered a clean, focused landing page that gave SpaceHub a professional web presence and a clear entry point for new members.
